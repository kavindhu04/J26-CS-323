How the weights are derived (AHP)

Experts compare the six dimensions two at a time on the Saaty 1–9 scale ("how much more important is A than B for trust in a credit-scoring AI?"). Answers are combined by geometric mean, the priority vector gives the weights, and the consistency ratio (CR) must be below 0.10.     Scoring: each indicator is rated Absent (0), Partial (1) or Full (2) against the scoring guide and a recorded evidence item (EP-01 to EP-18 in the simulated evidence pack). A dimension score is the indicator mean scaled to 100; the Cyber Trust Index is the weighted mean of the six dimensions. Bands: ≥75 Approve · 60–74 Monitor · 40–59 Restrict · <40 Reject. Indicators are grounded in MAS FEAT, the NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001. Illustrative tool for PP1 — the system and its evidence are simulated, not a real institution's data.
