Scenario testing

Each scenario is a simulated combination of trust, risk and compliance results with its verdict fixed in advance. The tool decides each one and checks the result. The sensitivity column changes each criterion weight by ±10% and ±20%, one at a time, and checks whether the verdict holds.

How the weights are derived (AHP)

Experts compare the three criteria two at a time on the Saaty 1–9 scale ("for approving a banking AI system, how much more important is A than B?"). Answers are combined by geometric mean, and the consistency ratio must be below 0.10. The judgements below are illustrative placeholders; they get replaced by the expert review responses.

Method (iteration 1): inputs are normalised so higher always means better governed — Trust = CTI, Risk = 100 − CREI, Compliance = CCS. Governance score = Σ weight × normalised value, with AHP weights. Score bands: ≥75 Approve · 60–74 Monitor · 40–59 Restrict · <40 Reject. The final verdict is the stricter of the score band and any override rule. Accountability follows the Three Lines model: 1st line owns and fixes, 2nd line oversees and challenges, 3rd line (internal audit) gives independent assurance. ATLAS mitigation IDs are indicative and should be checked against the current release. Inputs are simulated until integration with Components 1–3.
