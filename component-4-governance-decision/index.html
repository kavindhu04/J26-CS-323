How the weights are derived (AHP)

Experts compare the three criteria two at a time on the Saaty 1–9 scale ("for approving a banking AI system, how much more important is A than B?"). Answers are combined by geometric mean, and the consistency ratio must be below 0.10. The judgements below are illustrative placeholders; they get replaced by the expert review responses.

Method (iteration 1): inputs are normalised so higher always means better governed — Trust = CTI, Risk = 100 − CREI, Compliance = CCS. Governance score = Σ weight × normalised value, with AHP weights. Score bands: ≥75 Approve · 60–74 Monitor · 40–59 Restrict · <40 Reject. The final verdict is the stricter of the score band and any override rule. Accountability follows the IIA Three Lines Model (Statement of Position, July 2026): 1st line owns and fixes, 2nd line oversees and challenges, 3rd line (internal audit) gives independent assurance. Each action is assigned with RACI (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted = 2nd-line reviewer, Informed = 3rd-line assurance). Checks (iteration 1): CHK-1 every action fully assigned (accountability coverage, target 100%); CHK-2 each person sits on the right line; CHK-3 reviewer and auditor are different people from the doer and owner; CHK-4 the verdict signer is not the system owner or an action owner. Decision authority rises with the verdict (Approve/Monitor: AI Governance Committee; Restrict: CRO; Reject: Board Integrated Risk Management Committee). Closing actions does not change the verdict by itself; the verdict changes only when C1–C3 reassess with the new evidence. The owner register is fictional.
