    Formula: CCS = Σ(Wᵢ · Aᵢ · Sᵢ) / Σ(Wᵢ · Aᵢ) × 100. W = control weight (3 = high impact, 2 = standard; iteration 1, to be validated by expert review and sensitivity analysis). A = 1 if the control applies to the asset in the current scope, else 0. S = Met 1 · Partially met 0.5 · Not met 0. Relationship taxonomy: Equivalent, Overlapping, Complementary, Unique, Conditional, Potentially Conflicting. Source references were checked online on 21 October 2026 (simulated evidence pack, Section 8); ISO/IEC 42001 is cited at Annex A control-area level because the standard's full text is paid. A mapping is not evidence of compliance: status comes only from the evidence assessment. Illustrative tool for PP1 — the asset, evidence and statuses are simulated, not a real institution's data.
