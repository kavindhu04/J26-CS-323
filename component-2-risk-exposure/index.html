    Method (iteration 1): residual exposure per threat = residual likelihood × impact (1–25). Layer exposure = mean of its included threats, scaled to 100. Threat index = layers averaged with equal weight. Context modifier = 0.7 + 0.1 × (weighted context score − 1), so a 1–5 context gives ×0.7 to ×1.1. CREI = threat index × context modifier, capped at 100. Bands: 0–24 Low · 25–49 Medium · 50–74 High · 75–100 Critical. Override rules can raise the band, never lower it (Table 4.3). Context weights are the Table 4.2 defaults; the delivered weights come from the two-round Delphi. Threat vocabulary from MITRE ATLAS, NIST AI 100-2e2025 and the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications; ATLAS IDs are indicative and should be checked against the current release. Illustrative tool for PP1 — the asset, ratings and controls are simulated, not a real institution's data.
